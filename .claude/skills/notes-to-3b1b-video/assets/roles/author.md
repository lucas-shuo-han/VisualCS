# Brief: author

Write the design and boards for <unit> from `<notes>`. Cheap builders will copy every word and build every picture as written, with no taste and no licence to change anything: leave nothing open. No code, no renders.

Read: `K/SKILL.md` (stages 1–2 are yours), `K/references/narration-writing.md`, `derivation-episodes.md`, `visual-patterns.md`; skim the header of `K/scripts/manim_kit.py`.

Deliver in `<unit folder>`:
0. `PLAN.md` (`K/assets/plan_template.md`): episode table + design per episode. Total minutes come from the coordinator; ask if missing. Stop for the go.
1. `series.py` from `K/assets/strict_series.py`.
2. Two pilot boards (`K/assets/board_template.md`): easiest and hardest episode. Stop; rules from their review come back to you.
3. Then the remaining boards, to those rules.

On every board:
- Narration final: linked sentences of 10–25 words, 2–4 per beat, numbers as words, lone letters spoken as words.
- Each beat: what is on stage and what changes on which exact phrase, with positions (block centres, widths), what stays and goes at a scene change. Figures lost from the notes are rebuilt and marked.
- Don't ask for what the builder's check rejects: a highlight as a second rectangle (recolour the border), a line through text, two lines between the same points, a words-only beat, a beat >14 s with <2 changes or >26 s with <3, anything below y = −2.9.
- No skipped step: notes' "clearly" / jumps worked out and listed under "Added to the notes". Check small cases before a general claim, and show them.
- Every fact with its notes line and all conditions; own memory marked or left out.
- ~140 words per minute; count words, split rather than run long. `series.py`'s `sub` is spoken: write it as narration.

Final message: files, words per episode, what was left out and why, every departure from the notes.
