# Teaching (author)

## Design, per episode
Notes state results in final order and skip why a learner would want them. Supply that:
- the question, and why a viewer would care
- the obvious attempt, failing visibly on real numbers; the real idea is the repair
- one concrete example carrying the episode
- the aha, and the picture that makes it
- what is left out, and why

## Coverage
List every substantive item of the notes (concept, rule, table, example, exercise); map each to an episode and beat, or omit it with a reason (`COVERAGE.md`). Keep the notes' own examples and numbers. Length from content: one idea 3–4 min, a chapter 6–8; total minutes agreed with the user before splitting.

## Story
- Each sentence uses only what the previous one produced.
- Try, fail, try again, succeed, then name the pattern. Failures are content.
- No spoilers, no teasers, no "we require": conditions come from the goal.
- Case before rule, reason before definition, name after the thing is seen, overview last.
- Easy case first, by trying numbers; find what decides the outcome; reduce each new case to the last.
- Close the argument: whole region, edges. The last scene answers the first question.
- Past ~25 min, split where the viewer has a new question; each episode ends on the next one's question.

## Hard steps
- Compare the quantities themselves, the deciding factor named on screen.
- Arithmetic in steps, one line per sentence; the argument written on screen as spoken. A proof told only in pictures reads as "derivation missing".
- Name what is used without proof; keep that list short.

## Rigour
- No skipped step: the notes' "clearly" and two-line jumps are worked out and marked as added.
- Every number computed; every fact with its notes line and all its conditions; own memory marked.
- Notes and handwritten derivations contain errors: correct them and list each correction.
- Watch for: an approximation where the exact form is as short, a general claim with an unstated exception, a loose word, a "because" that is only assertion.
